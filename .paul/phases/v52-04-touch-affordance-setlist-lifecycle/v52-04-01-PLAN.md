## Purpose

Issues 5 + 7 are the band-onboarding gate-clearers that remain after v52-02 fixed input/keyboard and v52-03 fixed sync recovery. Daniel's iPad workflow currently:

- Cannot find the kebab on a setlist card without trial-and-error tapping (tap-to-hover doesn't always trigger; the kebab is invisible at rest on iPad).
- Cannot see the "Plan Service" placeholder inside an empty Friday/Saturday calendar cell at all on iPad (opacity-0 + no hover state on touch).
- Sees "Edit Setlist" as a muted gray button next to a brand-accented "Clone" button → reads "Clone" as the primary action, but the actual primary task in 90% of week-to-week sessions is **edit last week's clone** (per memory: "Clone last week's setlist → tweak a few songs (90% same week to week)"). The CTA hierarchy is inverted.

Direct PROJECT.md value mapping: **"bulletproof and easy and intuitive" before onboarding band**. Tapping ghost-affordances and misreading button hierarchy both break "intuitive."
