<objective>
## Goal
Kill the `/perform` listing **CLS 0.187** (FIX-1 from the v11.3-04-01 characterization). Root cause: the anon QR sign-in card (`PublicSetlistListing.tsx:189` — `{!user && !authLoading && (<section class="bg-card …">)}`) is **absent on first paint** (`authLoading` starts true) and **mounts ~380px tall above the Upcoming/Past list sections** once client auth resolves, shoving both lists down. Reserve the card's vertical slot during `authLoading` so the late mount does not shift siblings → bring `/perform` CLS from 0.200 (field p75) / 0.187 (synthetic iPad) down to ≤ 0.1 (target ~0.02, the `/setlists` comparator).

## Purpose
`/perform` is the band's highest-traffic surface and the public gig-discovery landing. CLS 0.2 is "needs-improvement" on the band's primary screen — content jumps under the reader's eyes as they go to tap a service. This is the deterministic, low-risk half of BUG-2 (the shift reproduces every load, cold and warm, independent of network) and the clean win.

## Output
- `PublicSetlistListing.tsx`: the sign-in region reserves its height during `authLoading` so the lists render at their final position immediately; the real card swaps in (anon) or collapses (authed, rare on this public surface) without moving the lists.
- `QRSignIn.tsx`: defense-in-depth — a consistent min-height across its internal render states (registering/active/signing-in/error) so internal transitions inside the reserved card don't reflow.
- A synthetic CLS regression test asserting `/perform` cumulative layout-shift < 0.1 at the 820×1180 iPad viewport, citing the web-vitals `/perform` CLS cell.
</objective>
