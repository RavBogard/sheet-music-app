## Goal
Redesign the schedule page so "all upcoming services" is the default view for everyone, with an optional calendar toggle. Simplify from 3 tabs to 2 views.

## Purpose
Musicians should land on a clear list of all upcoming services — not a personal "My Schedule" that may be empty. This matches the weekly workflow: Rabbi Daniel posts setlists, everyone sees the full schedule.

And the old 3-tab bar (My Schedule / Planning / Overview) is removed
