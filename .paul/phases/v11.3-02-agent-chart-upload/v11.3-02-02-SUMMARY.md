### Refinement

**1. Append is NOT separately rate-limited (plan said "mirror finalize").**
- **Why:** Per-chunk rate-limiting would exhaust the 10/min upload-tier cap on any multi-chunk file (a 5 MB chart ≈ 100 chunks), defeating the feature. The operation is already authorized at `begin`; `append` re-checks the role gate (revocation mid-flow) but is not metered. `begin` + `commit` (via finalize) carry the rate-limit. Documented in the module + tool descriptions.

### Deferred Items

- **finalize_chart_upload (signed-URL path) does not org-stamp its result** — a pre-existing gap shared by the signed-URL flow. This plan stamps only the new chunked commit. Close in a small follow-up (add `org` to `finalizeChartUpload` + pass `orgFrom(extra)` in its handler) — low-risk, but out of scope here to keep the signed-URL path byte-stable.
