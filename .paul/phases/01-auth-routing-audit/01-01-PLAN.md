<objective>
## Goal
Audit the full auth lifecycle and document all root causes for the three known regressions: (1) Monitor page redirect after fresh login, (2) buggy login popup/redirect cascade, (3) avatars not displaying. Produce a structured audit report with root causes, affected files, and fix recommendations.

## Purpose
Phase 2 (Auth Flow Rebuild) depends on knowing exactly what's broken and why. Fixing without auditing risks whack-a-mole — patching symptoms while missing root causes. This audit gives Phase 2 a surgical target list.

## Output
`.paul/phases/01-auth-routing-audit/01-01-AUDIT-REPORT.md` — structured findings with root causes, affected files, and recommended fixes.
</objective>
