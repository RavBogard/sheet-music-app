### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Correctness] Resolver emitted `date: null` from raw Firestore Timestamps**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (emulator qualify) — AC-1 newest-first sort returned insertion order.
- **Issue:** `isoOf` only passes through strings, but raw `doc.data()` dates are Firestore `Timestamp` objects → `date`/`eventDate` came back null (would also be null in production), and the date-based sort degenerated to insertion order.
- **Fix:** Route doc data through `serializeSetlist(doc.id, data)` (deep-serializes Timestamps → ISO strings) before reading `name`/`date`/`eventDate`/`sourceTemplateId` — exactly what the v11.7-03 sibling does.
- **Files:** `src/lib/mcp/tools/setlists.ts`
- **Verification:** re-ran both emulator suites → 24/24 green; AC-1 order now [S2-crc, S-crc].
