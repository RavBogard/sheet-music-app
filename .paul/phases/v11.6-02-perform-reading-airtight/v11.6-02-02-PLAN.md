<objective>
## Goal
Make **text/plain chord-chart reading airtight in Fit mode on the 11" iPad** —
the dominant surface for the two Camp Sabra weekend sets (both all-text/plain
→ `TextScoreViewer`). Specifically: (a) never shrink the font below a
legibility floor and never clip a long chorded line with no way to reach the
right edge (WS-03); (b) keep transposed chords aligned over their syllables
instead of drifting (WS-04); (c) give the in-chart controls iOS-sized touch
targets (WS-20).

## Purpose
At Camp Sabra the band reads these charts off iPads at music-stand distance.
Fit mode currently computes the auto-fit font from text-only lines (treating
every chord-lyric line as a constant 40 chars), so a long chorded line either
renders too large and **clips with no horizontal scroll** (`overflow-x-hidden`)
or, on the `min(15px, …)` path, shrinks **below readable size with no floor**.
Transposing (e.g. C→Db) widens a chord past its lyric slice and **pushes every
following column right**, so chords drift off the words. These are direct
"can't read the chart in service" defects on the highest-weight surface.

## Output
- `src/components/music/text-score-layout.ts`: pure, unit-tested helpers for
  the Fit-mode width measurement + font-size calc (extracted so the math is
  testable without rendering — jsdom can't compute `cqi`/`clamp`).
- `src/components/music/TextScoreViewer.tsx`: true line-width measurement +
  legibility floor + scroll-instead-of-clip (WS-03); chord cell that does not
  expand its column (WS-04); ≥44px control touch targets (WS-20).
- Unit tests for the layout helpers + DOM-structure assertions for the
  alignment/touch-target changes. Real-iPad legibility/alignment → UAT-PENDING.
</objective>

<acceptance_criteria>

## AC-1: Long chorded lines stay legible and reachable in Fit mode (WS-03)
```gherkin
Given a text chart with a long chord-lyric line (wider than the text-only lines),
  e.g. the Camp Sabra Staff Concert set, opened in Fit mode at 820px
When the viewer auto-fits the font
Then the auto-fit size is computed from the TRUE widest rendered line
  (chord-lyric lines included — not a constant 40)
And the font never renders below a legibility floor (>= ~11px at zoom 1.0)
And when content is still wider than the container at the floor, the chart is
  horizontally SCROLLABLE (no permanently clipped/unreachable right edge).
```

## AC-2: Transposed chords stay aligned over their syllables (WS-04)
```gherkin
Given a text chart rendered in Fit mode
When the user transposes so a chord becomes wider than its lyric slice (e.g. C→Db, G→F#m)
Then the chord-lyric column width is governed by the LYRIC slice, so a wider
  chord does not push following columns rightward
And lyric alignment across the line is preserved (chords may visually crowd but
  do not cumulatively drift off their words).
```

## AC-3: In-chart controls meet the iOS touch-target floor (WS-20)
```gherkin
Given the TextScoreViewer control bar (Fit/Wrap toggle, zoom out/in) on iPad
When a musician taps a control
Then each interactive control is at least 44x44px
And the control bar does not obscure the last lines of the chart (adequate
  bottom clearance below the scrollable content).
```
