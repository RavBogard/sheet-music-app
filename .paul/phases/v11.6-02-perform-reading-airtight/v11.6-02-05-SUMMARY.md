## Accomplishments

- **WS-22 de-risked from "inconclusive defect" to "harness false-alarm + proof":** the stress sweep's `.or(...).first()` grabbed the `display:none` off-breakpoint transpose trigger (mobile trigger on landscape ≥lg / desktop on portrait <lg) → `.click()` waited on an invisible element → the recorded 6s timeout. Fixed to a `:visible` selector and proved live that transpose opens + applies on both orientations and both chart types.
- **WS-14 fit-page:** new pure `computeFitPageWidth` (fit-width vs fit-height-to-viewport, with safe fallbacks) + ephemeral `fitMode` store slice (default 'width', reset on every chart transition) + PDFViewer container-height tracking and first-page aspect-ratio capture via `PDFPageWrapper onLoadSuccess`.
- **WS-18 / WS-26:** zoom % always visible; the readout is honestly "Reset zoom to 100%"; a PDF-only fit-mode toggle (StretchHorizontal ↔ Scan, primary-tinted in page mode) sits in the zoom group.
- Kept the re-runnable stress sweep honest: updated its zoom-fit cell to the shipped labels + added fit-toggle coverage.
