# Phase v11.6-02 Plan 02: Text Fit-mode reading airtight — Summary

**Text/plain chord charts (the two Camp Sabra sets' surface) are now airtight in Fit mode on iPad: the auto-fit measures the true widest line so long chorded lines no longer clip, a legibility floor + horizontal scroll replace shrink-to-illegible, transposed chords stay aligned over their syllables, and the controls meet the 44px touch floor.**

## Accomplishments

- Killed the WS-03 clip: the auto-fit denominator previously scored every chord-lyric line as a constant 40, so wide chorded lines rendered too large and clipped (the container was `overflow-x-hidden`). Now the true widest line drives the fit, with an 11px legibility floor and `overflow-x-auto` as the reachability escape.
- WS-04: a transposed chord wider than its lyric slice no longer stretches the flex column and drifts the whole line — the lyric governs width; wide chords crowd visually but stay put.
- WS-20: 32px → 44px controls + aria-labelled zoom buttons.
- Extracted the Fit-mode math into a pure, unit-tested module.

## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| `w-0` chord scoped to Fit mode via a `renderChunkCol` param; both call sites use explicit arrows | Wrap mode's BUG-7 word grouping relies on the chord contributing width; `.map(fn)` would also pass the array as the new 3rd arg (truthy) | Wrap mode byte-unchanged; no accidental width-neutral in wrap |
| Add `aria-label` to icon-only zoom buttons while resizing | ui-ux-pro-max a11y guidance (icon-only buttons need labels) + makes WS-20 testable by accessible name | Minor in-scope addition; improves a11y |
