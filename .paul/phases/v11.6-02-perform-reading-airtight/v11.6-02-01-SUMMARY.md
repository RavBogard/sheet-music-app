## Accomplishments

- Closed the phase-02 P0 (WS-01): the queue filter at three effects (PDFOverlay.tsx) excluded `type!=='song'` rows, so opening a bonded prayer row resolved `queueStart=-1` → `Math.max(0,-1)=0` → the sync effect navigated to song 1. Widening the predicate to any fileId-bearing row fixes it.
- Fixed WS-09 (same -1→0 root cause): a live-director queue rebuild that drops/replaces the follower's row no longer snaps them to song 1.
- WS-02 one-line CSP fix unblocks Firestore WebChannel long-poll hydration on WebKit/Safari.
- DRY'd the triplicated inline filter into one exported, unit-tested helper.

Offline pre-caching is **phase v11.6-03** territory — revisit there (consider preserving the real fileId in `toQueueItem` when offline pre-caching of non-song charts matters).
