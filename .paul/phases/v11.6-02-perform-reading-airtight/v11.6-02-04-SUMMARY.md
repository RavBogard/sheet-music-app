## Accomplishments

- Closed the WS-05 stuck-spinner: the existing 60s timeout only covered the fetch; a render-stage watchdog now bounds rendering and surfaces Retry. A `width > 0` guard kills the transient blank `<Page width={0}>` on iPad rotate-during-load.
- WS-07: multi-page charts expose a "Page X of N" indicator (IntersectionObserver-tracked, single-page hidden) so page 2+ below the fold is discoverable.
- WS-16: a rotate-scale resize restores a fresh retry budget — a stuck chart recovers on rotate, no leave-and-re-enter.
- Extracted the reliability DECISIONS into a pure, unit-tested module.
