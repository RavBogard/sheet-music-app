## Accomplishments

- **Diagnosed the real root cause against deployed `origin/master`** (cwd on master, in sync, files byte-identical): the report's id-shape/UUID hypothesis was wrong — `loadEditableSetlist` resolves by direct `.doc(id).get()` and handles UUIDs fine. The break was that `propose_setlist_changes` (index.ts:1331) + `commit_staged_changes` (index.ts:1352) were the only two by-id setlist write tools never given the v11-02-03 `orgFrom(extra)` threading, so the org wall defaulted to `crc` and rejected BL setlists as not-found.
- **Fixed both tools** + closed a latent commit-time isolation gap (`commit_staged_changes` previously read the setlist with no org check at all).
- **Locked in regression coverage** so a future missing-org-arg can't silently re-break tenant isolation here.
