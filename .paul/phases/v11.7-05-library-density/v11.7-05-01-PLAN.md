<objective>
## Goal
Densify the library browse row (`LibraryFileRow`, rendered by `SongChartsLibrary` at
`/library` — both the band/consumer browse and the in-app Library tab are this one
surface) into a Logic-Pro track-list-style row, and surface the chart's **composer**
(from the filename parenthetical) as a dimmed sub-label. Text-only — NO thumbnails.

## Purpose
F3 library density (Daniel 2026-06-22): make charts identifiable at a glance with
max-density text rows. Verify-first found the row already carries key/bpm/topics/recency
but renders SPACIOUS (`text-xl` bold title, `h-10` icon, `py-4`) and hides recency/topics
on mobile — and the composer is mashed into the title. This phase tightens the row and
splits out the composer.

## Output
- `src/lib/library/chart-composer.ts` — pure `splitChartComposer(name)` → `{ title, composer }`.
- Denser `LibraryFileRow` with a dimmed composer sub-label + compact key/recency visible at all breakpoints.
- Tests; no new data source, no thumbnails, no deploy.
</objective>

### Design guidance applied (from /ui-ux-pro-max, baked into Task 2 / AC-2)
- **Tap target ≥44px:** keep `min-h-11` on the row Button. Density comes from smaller font/icon/internal padding, NOT a sub-44px row.
- **Primary title ≥16px on mobile (readable-font-size):** drop the `sm:text-xl` oversizing and the `font-bold` → `text-base font-medium`; do NOT shrink the title below `text-base`.
- **Composer contrast (4.5:1):** dimmed = `text-muted-foreground`, smaller (`text-xs`/`text-sm`) — visually subordinate, never competing with the title; omit when absent.
- **Hover:** color/opacity transition, not a layout-shifting scale on the row (existing icon `group-hover:scale-110` is icon-only → OK).
- **Icons:** lucide SVG (already used); no emoji.
