<objective>
## Goal
Restore the Firestore `isMember()` gate on setlist reads that was temporarily relaxed to `isSignedIn()` in commit `0b10ecf` during the v4.3 musician lockout. With the P9/P10 drift-repair chain now guaranteeing the custom claim syncs shortly after sign-in, the original rule is safe again — and its restoration closes the last piece of the audit debt.

## Purpose
`0b10ecf` was an incident hotfix: musicians whose role lived only in `users/{uid}.role` and not yet as a `token.role` custom claim were getting locked out of setlist reads. Relaxing to `isSignedIn()` opened setlist reads to any authenticated user, including those with role=`pending`.

With the repairDrift chain (P10-03) and router.refresh (P10-02), the claim now reliably syncs within seconds of sign-in or role promotion. Pending users still can't read setlists, as intended. Storage rules already use `isMember()` directly; this aligns Firestore to the same posture.

## Output
- `firestore.rules` setlists read rule: `allow read: if isMember() || isOwner(resource)` (not `isSignedIn()`).
- The `isOwner(resource)` guard is kept so a user's own setlist remains readable even during the brief claim-sync window after they create one.
- The existing create/update/delete rules are unchanged — they already use role + ownership semantics.
- Deploy rules via Firebase CLI or leave for user deployment (document which).
- `10-04-SUMMARY.md`.
</objective>
