<objective>
## Goal
Kill the two dashboard onboarding cards that can stack and replace two hand-rolled / `window.confirm` dialogs with the app's `AlertDialog` primitive.

## Purpose
- Dual onboarding cards (`pending` user + `approved-without-instrument` user) share ~80% of their structure and can render simultaneously during role transitions — the literal stacking bug called out in the Phase 4 roadmap.
- `TransferSetlistDialog` is a hand-rolled `<div className="fixed inset-0 bg-black/80…">` overlay that bypasses the accessible AlertDialog pattern used everywhere else.
- `SetlistHistoryPanel.handleRestore` guards a destructive action with a native `window.confirm` — inconsistent with the rest of the app's destructive-action pattern (all use AlertDialog) and inaccessible on mobile/tablet.

Bigger Phase 4 items (triple-modal chain: SearchOverlay + AddSongsModal + AddToSetlistSheet; inline badges to replace routine success toasts; error-toast sweep; z-index tokens; INSTRUMENTS registry unification) stay out of this plan and ship in subsequent 04-0N plans — they each deserve their own vertical slice.

## Output
- New `src/components/dashboard/OnboardingCard.tsx` — single role-aware component that renders at most once regardless of role transitions.
- `DashboardClient.tsx` collapses both card blocks into one `<OnboardingCard />` invocation.
- `TransferSetlistDialog` becomes an `AlertDialog`-based component matching the Delete/Duplicate dialogs' shape.
- `SetlistHistoryPanel` grows a `RestoreConfirmDialog` replacing the native `confirm()`.
- Unit tests for OnboardingCard (stacking guard).
</objective>
