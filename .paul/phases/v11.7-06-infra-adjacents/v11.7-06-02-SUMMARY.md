## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| `org` param OPTIONAL on finalizeChartUpload (no default) | The chunked path stamps externally (line 1049); a default would double-stamp | chunked path untouched, no double-write |
