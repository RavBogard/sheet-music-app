<objective>
## Goal
Migrate the two email-sending route handlers (`/api/setlist/email-packets` and `/api/setlist/resend-email`) to read tracks via the shared `getTracksForSetlist` helper. Final v60-04 plan; closes the last server-side reader leakage point before v60-07 writer strip becomes safe.

## Purpose
Both email routes derive their email-body song list from `setlist.tracks || []` — stale for any hydrated setlist since v50-05. `email-packets` lists songs in the per-member packet-download email (the email that links into `print/personal`). `resend-email` re-sends the publish-notification body when initial delivery failed and recomputes its song list from the same stale source. With these two migrated, every server reader of "the live track list" routes through the helper, unblocking v60-07 (embedded-array writer removal) and v60-08 (legacy-branch cleanup).

## Output
- `email-packets/route.ts:61` chained reader migrated through `getTracksForSetlist`; chain decomposed into a typed local `tracks` then `.filter().map()` over it.
- `resend-email/route.ts:118` reader migrated through `getTracksForSetlist` with inline cast preserving the existing narrow shape used by the `songNames` builder (line 119).
- Single combined commit per v60-01/02/03/v60-04-01/v60-04-02 precedent.
- v60-04 phase LOOP COMPLETE after this plan — phase-completion transition follows.
</objective>
