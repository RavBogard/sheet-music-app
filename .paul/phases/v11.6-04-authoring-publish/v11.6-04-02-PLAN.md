<objective>
## Goal
Close WS-21: the QR session-approval (`PUT /api/auth/qr`) hard-requires the band
`role` to be present as a **token claim**, so a musician whose role lives in their
Firestore user doc but hasn't yet propagated to their ID token (claim-lag) is hit
with a 403 and has no in-flow remedy. Add a **user-doc role fallback** so an
actually-approved band member can approve a shared-iPad sign-in even while their
claim is stale — without loosening *who* is allowed (musician/band_leader/admin only).

## Purpose
Shared 11" iPads are how the band gets onto Perform at Camp Sabra this weekend. A
musician who was recently approved (or had their role set via `/api/admin/set-role`,
which explicitly can leave `claimsUpdated=false` — role in Firestore, old/absent
claim in the token) currently cannot device-approve until their token refreshes.
That is a real airtight gap on the access path. The fix is a resilience change that
turns a false-negative 403 into a correct allow; it does not expand the allowed-role
set.

## Output
- `PUT /api/auth/qr` resolves an **effective role** = the `role` claim if present and
  allowed, else the caller's `users/{uid}.role` Firestore field; gates on that.
- A genuine non-member (no allowed role in claim OR doc) still gets 403.
- Regression cases in the existing QR route test.
- SUMMARY at `.paul/phases/v11.6-04-authoring-publish/v11.6-04-02-SUMMARY.md`.

## STOP-GATE
This is an **auth/permission-surface change** (`autonomous: false`). It broadens the
set of callers who pass the QR approval gate (claim-lagged-but-real members).
Requires Daniel's approval before APPLY — per the H9 permission-change precedent.
