<objective>
## Goal
Make the band's publish-delivery + iPad sign-in path airtight by closing the two
non-permission gaps the stress sweep routed to phase 04: **WS-11** (the in-app
"setlist published" bell notification links to a dead route) and **WS-31** (the QR
sign-in page declares "expired" on a not-yet-registered session race). Both are pure
correctness/resilience fixes — no auth-policy change, no prod side-effect.

## Purpose
This weekend's three Camp Sabra / Juneteenth sets (Jun 19–20) are about to be
published to real band members on shared 11" iPads. A musician who taps the in-app
bell lands on a 404 instead of the setlist; a phone that scans the iPad QR a beat too
early sees a false "expired" and gives up. Both directly break "the band gets the
right charts on their iPads." Fixing them is the deliver-airtight half of the
milestone's final phase. (WS-21, the QR approval claim-lag, is a permission-surface
change → deferred to Plan 02 as a STOP-gate; the live publish send → Plan 03.)

## Output
- WS-11: both in-app notification `link:` fields point to the live
  `/perform/setlist/{id}` route (matching the already-correct push/SMS links).
- WS-31: the QR phone page distinguishes `404` (not-yet-registered → bounded retry)
  from `410` (genuinely expired → "expired"), so an early scan recovers.
- Regression coverage for each, tsc clean, `next build --webpack` clean.
- SUMMARY at `.paul/phases/v11.6-04-authoring-publish/v11.6-04-01-SUMMARY.md`.

