<objective>
## Goal
Fix two issues in the gig packet PDF: (1) swap Key and Lead columns so Key is 2nd and Lead is 3rd on the setlist cover page, and (2) exclude non-song items (readings, prayers, transitions, notes) from the charts section of the PDF.

## Purpose
The printed gig packet is the band's primary reference during services. Key needs to be immediately visible next to the song title (column 2), and non-song items cluttering the charts section wastes paper and confuses musicians flipping through pages.

## Output
Modified `src/lib/print-pipeline.ts` with corrected column order and filtered charts section.
</objective>
