  <action>
    The fragmentation: `parseIntoChunks` (:144) cuts the lyric at each chord's
    column index into independent `flex flex-col` chunks; in WRAP mode
    (`flex-wrap`, :276) those chunks break across lines mid-word, so a word with
    >1 chord above it splits ("Hallelujah" → "Hall"/"eluj"/"ah").

    Fix the splitter so a lyric WORD is never fragmented across wrappable chunks,
    while preserving monospace alignment:
    - Make chunk boundaries WORD-ATOMIC for wrap purposes: a chunk that contains
      part of a word must keep the whole word together (and the chords sitting
      above that word travel with it). Concretely, when building chunks, do not
      let a `flex-wrap` break fall inside a run of non-space lyric characters —
      group consecutive non-space lyric columns (and their overhead chords) into
      a single non-breaking unit (e.g. wrap each word-unit in an
      `inline-flex`/`whitespace-nowrap` container, or coalesce chunks that are
      not separated by a space in the lyric line).
    - FIT mode (`flex-nowrap w-max`) must remain column-accurate: chunks abut with
      their existing padded widths so chord↔lyric alignment is unchanged. If the
      simplest correct approach is to render fit-mode as two stacked
      `whitespace-pre` lines (raw chord line over raw lyric line), that is
      acceptable and MORE faithful to the monospace source — but verify CRC/known
      charts still align before choosing it.
    - Keep the XSS-safe React-text rendering (no raw HTML) and the existing
      transpose-on-chord behavior.
