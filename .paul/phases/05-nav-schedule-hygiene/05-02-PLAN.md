## Goal
Delete orphaned Next.js routes `/settings/users` and `/settings/sound`. Document that `SetlistDrawer` and `monitor-live/commands/pending` are confirmed-live and stay in place.

## Purpose
Both `/settings` leaf pages were unreferenced from any UI surface — `/settings/users` renders a generic "Admins Only" stub, `/settings/sound` is a duplicate host for `SoundSystemSection` (already rendered on `/manage`). Removing them shrinks the route map and eliminates confusing duplicate entry points before band onboarding.

The other two ROADMAP Phase 5 audit items (`SetlistDrawer`, `monitor-live/commands/pending`) were investigated and found in active use — they remain untouched. An AUDIT-NOTE.md records the rationale.
