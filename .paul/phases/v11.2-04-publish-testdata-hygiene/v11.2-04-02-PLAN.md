<objective>
## Goal
Close BUG-5 test-data hygiene on two surfaces: (1) extend `cleanup_all_test_data`
to ALSO sweep `setlists` where `isTest==true` regardless of owner (so a test
setlist authored under a REAL admin uid is removable), and (2) hide `isTest`
setlists from the authed `(main)` dashboard, mirroring the `/perform` filter.

## Purpose
Brothers Lazaroff stress-test (2026-06-09) left `isTest:true` setlists authored
under a real admin uid that (a) `cleanup_all_test_data` could not remove (it
only cascades `test-*`-OWNED data) and (b) cluttered the authed dashboard's
"Upcoming Shows" even though `/perform` already hid them. This makes the test
namespace fully self-cleaning and the two surfaces consistent.

## Output
- `cleanupAllTestDataCore` gains an additive, idempotent, full-sweep-only
  `setlists where isTest==true` pass (+ their tracks), reported in `aggregate`.
- A shared `isNonTestSetlist(s)` predicate reused by `/perform` AND the dashboard.
- Emulator + unit regression tests (incl. the self-inclusion path).
- SUMMARY at `.paul/phases/v11.2-04-publish-testdata-hygiene/v11.2-04-02-SUMMARY.md`.
</objective>

## Decision context — scope correction (verified, NOT spec-trusted)
The ROADMAP Phase v11.2-04 text says "sweep setlists/library_index where
isTest==true". VERIFIED against deployed code: `isTest` is a field on the
Setlist model ONLY. `library_index` and `songs` carry NO `isTest` field —
test charts are cleaned exclusively by OWNER-cascade (`CASCADE_FIELDS`:
`library_index.uploadedBy`, `songs.uploader` == a `test-*` uid). There is no
flag to sweep `library_index` on, and inventing one would mean editing every
chart-upload write path (unrequested scope creep). Therefore Part 1 is scoped
to `setlists` only; the `library_index` flag-sweep is dropped as
unimplementable-as-written and recorded as a known limitation (see boundaries).
