<objective>
## Goal
Make `preview_publish` surface **unbonded song rows** — `type:"song"` (or
untyped) tracks with no chart bond (`fileId` AND `songId` both null) — which
render blank in Perform yet today's preview reports `recommendation:"publish",
flaggedBonds:0`. Add an `unbondedSongCount` (+ `unbondedSongs[]`) signal and
escalate the recommendation to `review_first`. (BUG-4 from the BL stress-test.)

## Purpose
`preview_publish` is the chat-native "ready to publish?" gate Daniel relies on
before notifying the band. Its recommendation is derived only from `chartHealth`
(which probes BONDED rows) + `flaggedBonds` — so a song row with no chart at all
is invisible: the gate says "publish" and the band gets a blank chart in Perform.
`verify_setlist_charts` already flags these as `unbonded`; preview must not be
blind to them.

## Output
- `PreviewPublishResult` gains `unbondedSongCount: number` + `unbondedSongs:
  Array<{trackId, title}>`.
- `recommendation` escalates to `review_first` when `unbondedSongCount > 0`
  (unless already `hard_block`).
- Non-song chart-less rows (header/reading/prayer/transition/note) are NOT
  counted (intentionally chart-less).
</objective>
