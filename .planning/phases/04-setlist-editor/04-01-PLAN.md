<objective>
Transform the setlist editor from modal-based editing to phone-first inline editing with accordion expand, search-first song adding, auto-publish, and change notifications.

Purpose: This is the core UX transformation that makes the editor "faster than a spreadsheet" for phone-first editing. Every common edit (key, tempo, lead, notes) happens inline with zero modals.
Output: Inline accordion editing on all rows, search overlay for adding songs, auto-publish on save, musician notifications on significant changes.
</objective>
