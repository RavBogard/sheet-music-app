# Phase 1: Consolidate Performance Engines - Plan

**Status:** Ready for Execution
**Requirements Covered:** ARCH-01, ARCH-02, ARCH-03

## Overview

This plan details the steps to eliminate the legacy "footswitch" performance engine, resolving technical debt and establishing `/perform/setlist/[id]` as the single unified performance view. 

## Research Findings (gsd-phase-researcher)

1. **Component Dependencies:**
   - `PerformerView.tsx` and `FlowItemView.tsx` are strictly imported only within `src/app/perform/[id]/page.tsx`. Deleting them will not break other parts of the application.
2. **Keydown Event Listeners:**
   - Footswitch listeners (`ArrowRight`, `ArrowLeft`, `PageDown`, `PageUp`) are safely isolated to `PerformerView.tsx` and `FlowItemView.tsx`.
   - Other instances of `keydown` across the app are for accessibility (e.g., `Enter`/`Space` in dialogs or fader strips) or for typing.
3. **Legacy State in `useMusicStore`:**
   - The state slices `currentVisiblePage` and `syncedBroadcasterId` are heavily tied to the legacy live session synchronization. They appear in `lib/store.ts`, `PerformanceToolbar.tsx`, `PDFOverlay.tsx`, `PDFPageWrapper.tsx`, and `lib/live-session-firebase.ts` and can be safely scrubbed to clean up the legacy sync behavior.
