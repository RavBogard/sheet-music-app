## What you are confirming

One decision per **spelling**, not per setlist. "Bar'chu" appears in a dozen
services; you rule on it once and every one of them binds the same way.

A binding gives a row its liturgical identity: the moment it is, and the page
it is printed on in the legacy CRC booklet. It adds no row, removes no row and
reorders nothing — the row count is unchanged, by construction and by test.
Headers are never bound, and a page you typed yourself is never overwritten.

## The three tables

- **Clear** — an exact or near spelling, unambiguous. Say yes to the table and
  they all bind. Read the target column anyway: this is the list where a wrong
  one would be invisible.
- **Needs you** — real candidates, none safe alone. Usually one name that means
  two different pages. Pick, or leave unbound.
- **No match** — no candidate at all. Overwhelmingly song titles, which is the
  expected shape: `.live` is mostly the musicians' surface and its rows are
  mostly sung pieces. This is not thin data, it is what a band setlist is.

  Rosh Hashanah morning. The tool refuses that book rather than guessing at it.
- **Camp Sabra concerts and jams** (Staff Concert, Staff Party Jam, Havdalah &
  Kids Singalong) and **Mother's Day**. Not Shabbat services.
- The Camp Sabra Friday-night and Shabbat-morning SERVICES are included, read
  against the CRC booklets. They do not use those booklets, so treat their
