# Service census — what CRC actually does

A-W5' of `HANDOFF-CODE-LIVE-BIND-NOT-ADD-2026-09-15.md`. Read from every
Shabbat-family setlist with an event date on or after 2026-05-01, on
production, 2026-09-15.

**Nothing has been written to any template.** This is a proposal for what
refreshed templates could look like, not a change to them.

## How to read it

Daniel said the site's templates are not maintained, so this does not rewrite
them from a book — it reports what the services themselves contain. For each
family: every row appearing in at least half that family's setlists, in the
order it most often appears, with the liturgical identity A-W3' found for it.

Rows are grouped by their bare stem, so `Barchu (walkdown)`,
`Barchu (Friedman).pdf` and `Barechu` count as one row and the most common
spelling is shown. That is the right grain for a template: the template says
which moment, the service picks the arrangement.

**A small family is a weak signal.** Two setlists mean a row needs to appear
once to clear 50%. The count column is there so you can see that; treat any
family under four services as anecdote, not census.

# What this says

The families that are really families — Shabbat morning, Shir Shabbat, b'nai
mitzvah — have a stable spine, and it is a spine of SUNG pieces. That is the
expected shape and not thin data: `.live` is the musicians' surface, and the
spoken and read moments of a service mostly do not appear here because the
band is not doing them. A whole-service setlist is one deliberate step away
(`propose_service_frame`), not the default.

Two families are too small to census — one or two services each. They are
listed for completeness and should not drive a template.

Next step is yours: edit the family you want refreshed, and the handoff's
`create_template_from_setlist` / `update_template` applies it. Nothing here
moves on its own.
