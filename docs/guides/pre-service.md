# Before every service — Michael's checklist

| | Friday (service 6:15 pm) | Saturday (service 10:00 am) |
|---|---|---|
| Michael starts | 5:30 pm | 9:15 am |
| Band arrives | 5:45 pm | 9:30 am |

## 45 minutes before: power and iPads

- [ ] Make sure the **X32** is on.
- [ ] Turn on the **studio PC** and sign in to Windows. The bridge starts by itself. Wait for its
      tray icon to turn **green** (about a minute).
- [ ] Take the iPads off the cart. Each one should be **charged above 80%**. Charge any that aren't
      and give them to the musicians last.
- [ ] Turn on **Do Not Disturb** on each iPad, unless it's scheduled.
- [ ] On each iPad, open the **CRC Music** icon. Find tonight's service, marked **Today**, and tap
      **Perform** (or the service itself). Wait until the line in the header reads
      **"N/N charts on this iPad"** in green. If it's amber, tap **Save … charts offline** and wait.

## 30 minutes before: the band arrives

- [ ] Each musician picks up any iPad and checks **Settings**, which shows the name of whoever is
      signed in. If it isn't their name:
      **Log Out** → **Set up this device — sign in with phone** → they scan the QR code with their
      own phone → **Approve Sign-In**. The code lasts 5 minutes, so tap for a fresh one if it runs out.
      Their phone has to be signed in to centralreform.live already. A brand-new musician who sees
      "Your account is being reviewed" plays signed out for today. Text Daniel to approve them.
- [ ] They open tonight's setlist and play from the first song. Their transposition (Bb/Eb) follows
      their own account.
- [ ] Musicians on a wedge bus (Daniel on 5, David on 2) tap **Monitor**. It should say
      **Connected**, not "Bridge offline".
- [ ] Drew checks the engineer console: Bridge **Running**, and the limits are set.

## 5 minutes before

- [ ] Every iPad is on its stand, at the first song, with the screen on.

## If something's wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Bridge offline" or "Controls are paused" | Check the studio PC is on and signed in. Right-click the tray icon → **Quit Bridge**, then open **CentralReform Bridge** from the Start menu. If the icon is red, check the X32. |
| Amber "charts on this iPad" | Check the Wi-Fi, then tap **Save … charts offline**. |
| A musician can't sign in | Open the setlist signed out from the CRC Music icon. It works, but it won't follow live changes: tap **"Setlist updated — tap to refresh"** if it appears. |
| The screen goes to sleep | Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → **Never** for this service. |
| Anything else | Text Daniel what happened, when, and which iPad. |
