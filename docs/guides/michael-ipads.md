# Michael — getting the band iPads and the studio PC ready

The six band iPads live at CRC on the charging cart. Any musician can use any iPad: they sign in
with their own phone when they pick one up. Your job is to keep the iPads set up, charged and
updated, and to turn the studio PC on before each service and off after.

## Once per iPad (about 10 minutes each)

1. **Update iPadOS**: Settings → General → Software Update. It needs to be **18.4 or newer**: the screen
   stays awake by itself during a service only from 18.4 on. Then turn **Automatic Updates** off on that
   same screen. You install updates yourself, midweek only.
2. **Name it**: Settings → General → About → Name → `CRC Band 1` (2, 3 and so on). Put the same number
   on the case.
3. **Wi-Fi**: join the CRC network with Auto-Join on. Forget any guest networks.
4. **Auto-Lock**: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → **15 minutes**. On an iPad that can't
   reach 18.4, choose **Never**.
5. **Notifications**: Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb. Set it to turn on automatically on Friday
   5:30–8:30 pm and Saturday 9:15 am–12:30 pm, or turn it on by hand before each service.
6. **Multitasking**: Settings → Multitasking & Gestures (the name varies by iPadOS version). Choose
   full-screen apps only, and turn off the swipe gestures, so a stray swipe can't shrink the music.
7. **Add the app**: in Safari, open **centralreform.live/perform**, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**,
   and name it `CRC Music`. Put it in the dock. From now on, always open the music from this icon.
8. **Low Power Mode** off: Settings → Battery.

## Once on your phone (so the bridge can reach you)

Open **centralreform.live** in Safari and sign in with Google. Then tap Share → Add to Home Screen and
open the app from that icon. Go to **Settings → Notifications → Push**, turn it on and allow it. If the
mixer bridge isn't running about 30 minutes before a service, you and Drew get a "Monitor bridge needs
attention" alert.

## Once on the studio PC

- The **CentralReform Bridge** starts by itself when someone signs in to Windows. Its icon sits in the
  tray by the clock: **green** means it's fine, **orange** means it reaches the mixer but is behind, and
  **red** means it can't reach the X32.
- Ask Drew whether his engineer console says **"Watchdog installed"**. If it says "not installed",
  follow `bridge/watchdog/INSTALL.md` once (5 minutes), or text Daniel. The watchdog restarts the
  bridge within a minute if it ever closes.
- Bridge updates install themselves when the PC shuts down. You don't need to do anything.

## Midweek, about once a month

- Install iPadOS updates (never on a Friday or Saturday). Open the CRC Music icon afterwards to make
  sure it still opens.
- Wipe the screens, and check the chargers and cables.

## If something breaks

Text Daniel what happened, when, and which iPad (`CRC Band 3`).
