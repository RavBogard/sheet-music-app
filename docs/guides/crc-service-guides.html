Getting the band iPads and the studio PC ready

The six band iPads live at CRC on the charging cart. Any musician can use any iPad: they sign in with their own phone when they pick one up. Your job is to keep the iPads set up, charged and updated, and to turn the studio PC on before each service and off after.

Once per iPad about 10 minutes each

  1. Update iPadOS: Settings → General → Software Update. It needs to be 18.4 or newer: the screen stays awake by itself during a service only from 18.4 on. Then turn Automatic Updates off on that same screen. You install updates yourself, midweek only.
  2. Name it: Settings → General → About → Name → CRC Band 1 (2, 3 and so on). Put the same number on the case.
  3. Wi-Fi: join the CRC network with Auto-Join on. Forget any guest networks.
  4. Auto-Lock: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → 15 minutes. On an iPad that can't reach 18.4, choose Never.
  5. Notifications: Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb. Set it to turn on automatically on Friday 5:30–8:30 pm and Saturday 9:15 am–12:30 pm, or turn it on by hand before each service.
  6. Multitasking: Settings → Multitasking & Gestures (the name varies by iPadOS version). Choose full-screen apps only, and turn off the swipe gestures, so a stray swipe can't shrink the music.
  7. Add the app: in Safari, open centralreform.live/perform, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and name it CRC Music. Put it in the dock. From now on, always open the music from this icon.
  8. Low Power Mode off: Settings → Battery.

Once on your phone so the bridge can reach you

Open centralreform.live in Safari and sign in with Google. Then tap Share → Add to Home Screen and open the app from that icon. Go to Settings → Push Notifications, turn it on and allow it. If the mixer bridge isn't running about 30 minutes before a service, you and Drew get a "Monitor bridge needs attention" alert.

  • Install iPadOS updates (never on a Friday or Saturday). Open the CRC Music icon afterwards to make sure it still opens.
  • Wipe the screens, and check the chargers and cables.

Wedges, limits and the recording

Mix front of house in Mixing Station, the way you already do. The wedges also run through centralreform.live/monitor: musicians on a bus ride their own wedge from their iPad, and you control who's on which bus and how loud any wedge can go. Both apps talk to the same X32, so a move in one shows up in the other.

Once, on your iPad

  1. In Safari, open centralreform.live and sign in with Google using your engineer account.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the app from that icon, then go to Settings → Push Notifications, turn it on and allow it. If the bridge isn't running about 30 minutes before a service, you and Michael get a "Monitor bridge needs attention" alert.

The pieces of /monitor you'll use

  • Configure → bus assignments. Each bus lists the people on it. Tap Add to put a musician on a bus, or tap the × to remove them. Right now Daniel is on bus 5 and David is on bus 2. A new musician has to sign in to centralreform.live once before they show up in the list. If their account says it's waiting for approval, text Daniel.
  • Configure → Limits. This is the loudest any wedge can go. It's set to about −2 dB for channel sends and the wedge master, for every bus, and you can give one bus its own limits. The bridge holds every move made from an iPad or from Claude to your limit, and the musician sees "Limited to … by the sound engineer's limit". Going above 0 dB asks you to confirm first.
  • Engineer console (/monitor/engineer). Shows every bus with the people on it and the loudest channels. Use Ride this bus to take over someone's wedge: for example, when David says the bass is low in his wedge. The Bridge card says Running or Not running, when the bridge was last heard from, and whether the watchdog is installed.
  • Saved mixes. Musicians can save a mix and recall it next week. The app also keeps an "End of service" mix for each musician, under "After recent services". A double-tap on a fader puts it back at that person's last saved level. If they have no saved level, it goes to −6 dB, and never above your limit.

Limits don't apply to Mixing Station or the desk. They hold only the moves made from iPads and Claude.

Before the service with Michael's checklist

  • The engineer console says the bridge is Running, and a musician's Monitor screen says Connected.
  • The limits are where you want them.

After the service: the recording

Put the service mix in the Dropbox SERVICES folder:

  • Make a new folder named with the date and the service, for example 11.07_SAT MORNING or 11.13_FRI NIGHT.
  • Save the mix as an MP3 ending in _V1 (a WAV is fine too). For a second pass, use _V2.

Claude cuts the full service into one clip per song and attaches each clip to its chart. Recordings are public. Nothing plays during Perform.

If the bridge is down

Musicians see "Controls are paused. Your wedge sounds the same as before." Their wedges keep the last levels, and you can still mix everything in Mixing Station. To bring the bridge back:

  1. Check that the studio PC is on and signed in to Windows.
  2. Right-click the bridge's tray icon → Quit Bridge, then open CentralReform Bridge from the Start menu.
  3. If the icon stays red, check that the X32 is on and on the network.
