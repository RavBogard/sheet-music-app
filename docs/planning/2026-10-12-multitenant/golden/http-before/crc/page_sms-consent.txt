Skip to main contentCRC MusicSetlistsSign InCRC MusicSearchSetlistSchedule A project of Rabbi Daniel Bogardv11.7.0•Changelog•Privacy•Terms•AccessibilityTry the new experience (beta)LegalSMS Messaging Consent & Opt-InCentral Reform Congregation — CRC MusicHow SMS Opt-In WorksCRC Music musicians may opt in to receive SMS scheduling notifications by providing their phone number and enabling SMS notifications in their profile settings within the CRC Music application.Opt-in is voluntary and not required to use the CRC Music application. Musicians can use email and in-app notifications instead.Program DescriptionCentral Reform Congregation operates the CRC Music application, which includes an optional SMS notification service for musician scheduling. When musicians opt in, they receive text messages about:•Scheduling Assignments: Notification when you're assigned to play at a service•Reminders: Service reminders 48 hours before your scheduled performance•Confirmations: Confirmation when you accept or decline an assignment•Cancellations: Notification if a service or your assignment is cancelled•Schedule Changes: Updates to services you're assigned toConsent ProcessMusicians provide consent to receive SMS messages through the following process:1Sign in to the CRC Music application at www.centralreform.live2Navigate to Settings → Musician Profile → Scheduling3Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field4Toggle the SMS notification switch to ON5Save your profile settingsBy completing these steps, the musician affirmatively consents to receive SMS messages from CRC Music at the phone number provided.Message FrequencyMessage frequency varies based on how often the musician is scheduled for services. Typical musicians receive 2-8 messages per month. Messages are only sent in relation to your specific scheduling activity.Message and Data RatesStandard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. CRC does not charge for SMS messages sent through the Service.Opt-OutYou may opt out of SMS messages at any time by:•Replying STOP to any message received from CRC Music•Disabling the SMS toggle in your CRC Music profile settings•Removing your phone number from your profile settings•Contacting us at music@centralreform.liveAfter opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message. You will no longer receive SMS messages from CRC Music unless you opt in again.HelpFor help with SMS notifications, reply HELP to any message or contact us at music@centralreform.live.Data PrivacyMobile information, including phone numbers, will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.Phone numbers are stored securely and used solely for delivering scheduling-related SMS messages through our messaging provider (Twilio).
